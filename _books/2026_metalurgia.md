**Noções Básicas sobre Metalurgia** by Paulo Duarte is a clear and practical reference for anyone working with metallic materials. It covers the fundamentals of chemical, physical, and mechanical metallurgy — from atomic diffusion and solidification to fracture mechanics, fatigue, and wear — always connecting theory to real industrial practice.

The book is especially valuable for professionals involved in ultrasonic testing (UT/NDT), electromechanical maintenance, and metalworking, as it explains how microstructure, processing, and defects affect the behavior of steels and other alloys. Highly recommended if you want a solid, grounded understanding of metallurgy without unnecessary complexity.

**Note for a future edition:**  
While the content is excellent, a few editorial issues could be improved in a second edition. Some figures appear several pages ahead of the corresponding text, forcing the reader to flip back and forth. A few images are too small to be easily read, and there are occasional mismatches between the text references and the actual figures. Minor grammatical errors are also present. Addressing these points would make an already strong book even more pleasant to use.
